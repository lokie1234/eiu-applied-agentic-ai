## Typography

- Primary family: Inter, with system sans-serif fallback.
- Strong hierarchy: large navy headings, restrained uppercase gold eyebrows, compact metadata.
- Production guideline: keep body text >= 14-16 px on user-facing forms and >= 12 px only for secondary metadata.

## Geometry

- Cards: 18-24 px radius; hero surfaces up to 27 px.
- Buttons: pill/rounded, especially primary CTA.
- Inputs: 10-13 px radius with clear focus rings.
- Layout: desktop sidebar + wide content canvas; mobile bottom navigation.

## Surfaces and depth

- Navy gradients rather than flat dark fills.
- Gold/cream used sparingly for premium emphasis.
- Soft shadows; avoid dense skeuomorphic depth.
- Glass/blur effects only on limited surfaces; performance-sensitive pages should favor opaque surfaces.

## Motion

Main easing:

```css
cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1)
```

Spring accent:

```css
cubic-bezier(.2,.85,.32,1.22)
```

Preferred motion:

- 160-300 ms hover/press transitions.
- subtle translateY/translateX rather than heavy blur.
- progress growth, pulse, floating analytics cards, SVG line draw.
- always respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

Avoid:

- continuous heavy backdrop blur on many surfaces.
- one IntersectionObserver per small card.
- large parallax/3D effects on data-heavy screens.

## Login-page analytics identity

The final login scene combines:

- animated bar chart;
- animated trend line and pulse points;
- floating KPI cards;
- subtle data-stream dots;
- deep navy analytics canvas;
- warm gold data accents.

The objective is to make the login page immediately communicate Business Analytics without turning it into a decorative landing page.

## Brand behavior principles

1. Professional before decorative.
2. Data should be visually scannable in <5 seconds.
3. Gold marks priority, not every action.
4. Motion explains state and hierarchy; it should not compete with data.
5. Responsive behavior is part of the design system, not an afterthought.
6. Sensitive/destructive actions use explicit confirmation and differentiated danger styling.
